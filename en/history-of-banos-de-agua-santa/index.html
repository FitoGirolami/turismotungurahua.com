History of Baños de Agua Santa

The town's history is shaped by thermal waters, religious devotion, volcanic risk and the community's capacity to rebuild and reinterpret the territory.

Territorial memory

Water, mountain and faith

The history of Baños de Agua Santa cannot be separated from its thermal waters, religious devotion and the constant presence of Tungurahua volcano. These elements continue to shape the town's visual identity and tourism narratives.

Visitors often encounter the destination first through waterfalls or adventure, but the historical layer helps explain why water, risk, pilgrimage and resilience are recurring themes.

Interpretation matters

Religious narratives, oral tradition and documented historical events are different types of sources. They can coexist without being treated as identical evidence.

Key layers

A concise historical sequence

Thermal waters

Early identity

The town's name and reputation developed around hot water emerging from the volcanic landscape.

Religious memory

Virgin of Agua Santa

Devotion to the Virgin became central to local identity, pilgrimage and the interpretation of volcanic danger.

Modern history

1999 evacuation

Renewed volcanic activity led to the total evacuation of the town, a decisive event in recent collective memory.

Return

Community resilience

The return of residents and the reorganisation of the town became an important civic narrative.

Tourism

Transformation

Nature, hot springs, adventure and cultural attractions expanded as the town rebuilt its visitor economy.

Present

Multiple narratives

Historical memory now coexists with new artistic and commercial expressions within the tourism landscape.

The Basilica

Religious and heritage centre

The Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary of Agua Santa occupies a central place in the town. Its images and narratives communicate religious interpretations of volcanic events and community protection.

A careful visitor can appreciate its cultural importance while distinguishing devotional accounts from geological documentation.

Two complementary readings

Cultural: devotion, pilgrimage, images, vows and community memory.

Scientific: volcanic monitoring, hazards, evacuation and risk management.

Contemporary culture

New symbolic layers

In recent decades Baños has incorporated new artistic and themed experiences. Duende figures are part of this contemporary layer and should not automatically be presented as an ancient, homogeneous local tradition.
