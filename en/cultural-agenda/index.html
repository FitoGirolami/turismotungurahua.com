Cultural training

Ongoing programmes across the province

March–December · free

Ongoing cultural workshops

The Provincial Government maintains programmes in guitar, violin, singing, classical and contemporary dance, and folk dance. Activities run Monday to Friday in Ambato, Tisaleo and several parishes.

Important: these should not be treated as tourist drop-in activities. Contact the organiser directly to verify venues, availability, ages and participation requirements.

Baños de Agua Santa

Cultural experiences available throughout August

These are permanent experiences rather than one-day events. Their official sources are included to prevent confusion over location, authorship and opening hours.

Art garden · Baños

Visit La Aldea Mágica

A self-guided walk through paths, original sculptures, nature, lights and hidden texts. The experience offers an artistic and contemplative reading of the forest imaginary.

  • Monday–Thursday: 4:00–9:00 pm
  • Friday: 4:00–10:00 pm
  • Saturday: 11:00 am–10:00 pm
  • Sunday: 11:00 am–9:00 pm
  • Admission: adults USD 4; children and older adults USD 2

Art and craft · since 2013

Discover La Casa del Duende

A house-workshop and original art shop linked to the contemporary development of the duende imaginary in Baños. The visit forms part of the La Aldea Mágica experience.

It offers an opportunity to see handmade figures, learn the documented history of the project and understand its relationship with the city's cultural landscape.

Open agenda

How to propose an activity

Inclusion is free and does not imply sponsorship, institutional ranking or commission payments. Tungurahua activities are considered when they provide verifiable public information.

Minimum information

  • Name and short description.
  • Date, time, canton and address.
  • Organiser and public contact details.
  • Price or confirmation that it is free.
  • Official image and publication link.

Monthly update policy
