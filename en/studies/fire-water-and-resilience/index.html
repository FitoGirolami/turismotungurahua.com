Report v2.2 · January 2026

Purpose and method

The report reads Tungurahua as a territorial and cultural system. It separates documentary evidence, oral tradition, editorial interpretation and tourism narrative.

Its method combines chronology, institutional sources, web archives and comparative analysis of stories and tourism transformations.

Preserved record: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18216233. The Zenodo record preserves the document and associated materials.

Heritage and thermal waters

Water, mountain and symbolism

Hot springs have material, historical and tourism dimensions. The volcano is also interpreted through scientific, religious and symbolic frameworks that should not be treated as the same type of evidence.

Two readings

Scientific: volcano monitoring, hazards and risk management.

Cultural: devotion, memory, oral tradition and relationships with the mountain.

Themed tourism

From symbol to visitor experience

A symbol can become a handmade object, a place to visit, a narrative and a tourism product. The report observes this transition in the contemporary duende phenomenon and in the spread of “magical” attractions.

The central criterion is traceability: who created it, when it appeared, what documentation exists and how institutions participate.
