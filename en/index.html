Cultural tourism

Narratives, symbols and editorial responsibility

Baños brings together territorial legends, religious devotion, volcanic memory and contemporary cultural expressions. Clear interpretation requires a distinction between oral tradition, historical documentation, artistic creation and commercial reproduction.

This distinction does not weaken the travel experience. It makes it richer, more verifiable and more respectful of the people who created or transmitted each narrative and work.
