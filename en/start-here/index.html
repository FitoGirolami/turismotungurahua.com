Where to begin

A concise guide to deciding what to visit, how much time to allow and what information to check before setting out.

Quick orientation

Choose a route according to your available time

Baños can be explored in many ways. The most rewarding visits usually combine nature, rest and a cultural dimension.

1 day

The essentials

The Waterfall Route, Pailón del Diablo, one cultural experience and a viewpoint near sunset.

2 days

A balanced pace

Add hot springs, the historic centre and enough time to explore without rushing.

3 days

A broader reading

Add adventure activities, Runtún viewpoints and contemporary cultural experiences.

Before you leave

Four useful checks

1

Weather

The transition between the Andes and the Amazon brings rapid changes. Carry a light rain layer and shoes with good grip.

2

Opening hours

Check official sources, especially for hot springs, cultural venues and guided activities.

3

Safety

For adventure activities, choose formal operators, appropriate equipment and clear briefings.

4

Location

Check each project's official website to avoid duplicate or outdated map references.
